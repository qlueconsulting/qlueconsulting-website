Cognitive Intelligence & Data Synthesis

ScriptFlip

AI Video Script Generation & Teleprompter (iOS)
iOS App Store Active

ScriptFlip provides AI-powered video script generation and teleprompter services tailored for content creators, marketers, and video professionals. Utilizing Anthropic to turn topics and video outlines into engaging presenter scripts, ScriptFlip integrates a live on-screen teleprompter with local on-device data security.

Key Capabilities

Anthropic AI Generation

Real-time video script generation from user prompts, topics, and video summaries.

Integrated Teleprompter

Record seamlessly on iPhone with smooth, customizable scrolling pace and text display.

On-Device Privacy

Generated script history is stored locally on device. No AI model training on your data.
