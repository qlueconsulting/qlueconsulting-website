Frequently Asked Questions #

How are QLUE applications hosted?

Our software is engineered container-first. You can run our applications (including this very website) locally on Docker, Docker Compose, Kubernetes, or on your preferred cloud instance with minimal compute overhead.

Does AccoureAI transmit prompts to external servers?

No. When running AccoureAI in a local container or private server environment, your model prompts, file contexts, and data streams remain strictly inside your network perimeter.

How do I update applications?

Application updates are delivered as lightweight container tags. Running a standard docker compose pull && docker compose up -d will update your service without disturbing persistent volumes.

Privacy Policy #

Effective Date: October 7, 2026.

QLUE Consulting ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with our website, consulting services, and applications, including AccoureAI and ScriptFlip.

1. Information Collection & Self-Hosted Software

Our applications are built with a strict local-first philosophy. When you run containerized software from QLUE Consulting on your own server or private infrastructure:

  • We do not collect, inspect, or log your application inputs, script files, or model prompts.
  • We do not employ hidden background analytics, telemetry, or third-party behavioral trackers.
  • Data processed by ScriptFlip and AccoureAI remains residing entirely in your storage environment.

2. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you contact us for technical support, consulting requests, or licensing keys, we may collect:

  • Contact details (name, business email, organization name).
  • Information regarding your deployment configuration and error descriptions necessary to resolve support inquiries.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or operational information. Information is only utilized to deliver direct support, fulfill contractual consulting obligations, or comply with legal requirements.
