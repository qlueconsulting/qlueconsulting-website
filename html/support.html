How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Settings or Paywall screen inside ScriptFlip and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > ScriptFlip to modify or cancel.

Privacy Policy for ScriptFlip #

ScriptFlip (“we”, “our”, or “us”) provides AI-powered video script generation and teleprompter services. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

  • User-Provided Text: Topics, prompts, transcripts, or video summaries you submit to generate scripts. This data is processed in real-time to return scripts and is not used to train private AI models.
  • In-App Purchases: Subscription purchases are processed directly by Apple App Store via StoreKit and RevenueCat. We do not store or process your credit card numbers.
  • Diagnostics & Analytics: Anonymous crash diagnostics and basic app usage telemetry to improve performance.

2. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to deliver app functionality:

  • Anthropic: To generate video script copy from user prompts.
  • Supabase: For secure API routing and database operations.
  • RevenueCat: To manage in-app subscription entitlements.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Generated script history is stored locally on your device. You can clear your history or delete the app at any time to remove local data.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: brian@qlueconsulting.com.
